What remains unverified or incomplete
1. Photon acceptance and actual iMessage delivery are externally blocked.
   No delivery webhook/status reconciliation is implemented for this account;
   the app never equates acceptance with delivered. Conventional SMS and email
   have not been live-tested because their providers are not configured.
2. Railway deployment, Docker execution on Railway, multi-host load behavior,
   production staff provisioning and operational monitoring are unverified.
3. No new physical-camera fall or dangerous demonstration was requested.
   Actual hardware unplug, every camera/codec combination, browser autoplay
   blocking, multiple physical workstations and full network-outage scenarios
   have not all been exercised live. Permission/resource/recording failures
   have automated coverage, but not the entire requested browser failure matrix.
4. The real provider test used one fictional fall. All four original scenarios
   have automated and Atlas/browser checks, but separate real video/voice/
   delivery runs for every scenario and a complete live-camera urgent episode
   have not been performed. Stable/watching scenarios correctly do not issue
   urgent notifications under their original rules.
5. Browser-owned recording cannot recover footage lost before upload when a
   tab closes. Client completion retries exist, but persistent browser blob
   recovery, automatic reconciliation of uploaded-but-unlinked objects and a
   server timeout for a capture request that never reaches the browser remain
   implementation work. Such captures must not be treated as available.
6. S3 access expiry is enforced, but physical deletion/lifecycle and bucket
   access logging are not configured by the current limited IAM user. No
   production retention/deletion policy or ingestion-data archival job exists.
7. Privacy agreements, organizational authorization, clinical validation,
   comprehensive security/load review and real patient use are not approved or
   established. Demonstration success does not establish patient safety.
