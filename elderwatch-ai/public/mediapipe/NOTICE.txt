Locally served MediaPipe assets

Runtime: @mediapipe/tasks-vision 0.10.14, copied from the installed npm package.
vision_bundle.js is the unmodified vision_bundle.cjs, loaded by the classic
worker with an exports object. The WASM loader requires classic importScripts.
Runtime source/license: https://github.com/google-ai-edge/mediapipe
Upstream license: Apache-2.0.

Model: Google MediaPipe Pose Landmarker Full, float16, version 1.
Source: https://storage.googleapis.com/mediapipe-models/pose_landmarker/pose_landmarker_full/float16/1/pose_landmarker_full.task
Documentation: https://developers.google.com/edge/mediapipe/solutions/vision/pose_landmarker

These assets perform pose estimation. They are not a medically validated fall
or choking diagnostic model. Footage is processed in browser workers locally.
The app sends bounded landmark windows to its own authenticated observation
endpoint; no video frames are sent to Google for inference.
