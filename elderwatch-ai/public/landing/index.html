What does Senserra do today?+

The prototype combines camera views, pose-based observations, resident profiles, and incident review

This local preview uses recorded scenarios and fictional residents

Does it diagnose falls or choking?+

No — it surfaces possible concerns for human review

A gesture cannot confirm choking and clinical reliability has not been established

Who receives urgent SMS alerts?+

Healthcare providers and care staff receive alerts when they are assigned to the resident, active, and configured for urgent SMS notifications

The default message asks staff to open the secure dashboard for details, where they can review, acknowledge, and document their response

How is video handled?+

Pose processing runs in the browser

Configured services can receive landmark evidence, store incident clips, and send notifications

Where does the research come from?+

CDC and Alzheimer’s Association figures describe population-level risk and caregiver experience, not Senserra results

Sources were checked October 27, 2026 and are linked beside each figure
